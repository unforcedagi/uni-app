## Things the critics should know

- **Unconfirmed is the common state in the scratch data.** A task older than 3 days always starts there, and a related message from Uni after its own write (the usual announcement) also makes it unconfirmed; that follows the spec's rule, and "Answer anyway" is one tap.
- Items are matched by words, not meaning, so "related chat" stays a lexical check; it is only ever used to take a request *out* of "current".
