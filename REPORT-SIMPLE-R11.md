## Problems found and fixed on the way (real app)
- A first version of the long-question check flipped a row back and forth when a scrollbar moved the width by a few px: React error #185, a blank "Something slipped" screen, in the real app (not in Playwright). Fixed with a 40 px hysteresis and a reset only on a new text; checked in the final run.
- The Needs-you height budget left out the panel's own top bar, so with rows that shrink after measuring, "+2 more ›" was cut by the footer in the real app (page overflow of 26 px, measured). The budget now counts the bar, and the list measures again (up to six times in a row) if the panel still overflows. Final run: "+3 more ›" whole above the footer (`04`, `05`, `07`). Cost: the list is more conservative, so a row that fit by luck before can now sit behind "+N more ›" (two older specs needed a click on it).
