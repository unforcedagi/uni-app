## Things the critics should know
- **Uni reminders hours later unconfirm an ask.** Any Uni (or other) message after the 10-minute window that names the task, or two of its distinctive words, takes it out of confirmed, as before. COhere's three asks are an example. That is the rule as specified; it also means a room that re-posts its asks hourly keeps them unconfirmed.
- The "recheck: …" line the app writes once per launch (`uni.log`) can read `recheck: none` when the first settle happens before the rooms are read (seen in the probe run); R28's logger, unchanged. Not touched.
- `clauseForms` and `summaryClauses` are no longer used by the app (their R23/R26 unit tests still run); I left them rather than widen the round.
- `noneLine` also counts the "Checking N asks…" state as "not confirmed yet": while asks are being checked the empty line reads "No confirmed asks", never "Nothing needs you here."
