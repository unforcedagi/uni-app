# REPORT: Simple first, round 7 (T-124 r7): the coherence pass

On `feat/simple` from 52f3ca0. Nothing pushed. Not run in the real app or on live vault data.

## What changed, per item

1. **One model, one way.** Room lines are the room's real channel name from the rooms list: COhere shows `#cohere`, ATLAS `#atlas/build`, a node with no room of its own shows nothing (a quiet "in #boulder" is the row's hover title). `joinSlash` and the path-building walk are deleted (`treeRooms.roomView`). Header, placeholder, door chips, updates block and tree all read the same value. Node titles in the room header and the full page use the node's NAME (the tree's word), not the first heading of its note.
2. **Collapsible areas, persisted.** Every area (and any project with children) has a chevron button; it only folds. The name opens the room. Default: folded, except the branch holding the open room; a room opened from elsewhere reveals its branch. State is per device (`uni.tree.folds.v1`). A folded row shows the needs-you dot if anything under it needs you.
3. **Narrow sidebar.** 220px default, drag the right edge to resize (180 to 360, double-click resets, arrow keys work on the handle; `uni.sidebar.v1`), a hide toggle down to a 34px strip. The big "Rooms" heading is gone, replaced by a slim toolbar. Rows are 28px (36px with a room line). The phone tree keeps its touch-size rows.
4. **No tab strip.** Removed from the DOM and CSS. A place (room as its node, Uni, thread) replaces the place you were on; opening a room from the tree, the Rooms drawer, search, a note link, "Open #room" or a needs-you item all go through one resolver, so a room that a node owns always opens as that node (the one view, with the companion). The Journal keeps its own hidden tab so its page and any recording stay alive. A note or vault opens over the place with its Back, as before. Cmd/Ctrl+W closes a note or vault, never a place; Cmd+1..9 is gone.
   - **Back / Forward**: Alt+←/→ (left alone in text fields on a Mac, where Option+arrow moves by word) and two small buttons in the sidebar's toolbar (kept as a strip when the sidebar is hidden). A note over a place takes Back first. Up to 5 distinct previous places show under "Recent" at the bottom of the sidebar.
   - **Migration**: `uni.tabs.v1` is read once; only its active place is kept (or the last place under a note). From then on `uni.tabs.v2` holds places only, and the app reopens to the place you were in.
5. **Needs you is the feed.** Tasks that are Aaron's (owner, or waiting on him) and live and not snoozed, at Now or Soon (unsorted counts as Soon), plus open decisions; Someday is not shown; Now before Soon, then due date. Each row: the title is the action, then the task's `next` in full (wraps 4 lines, "More"/"Less" in place, URLs become their domain and stay clickable), "Waiting on you" and the due date as a quiet line. A row with neither a title of two or more words nor a `next` says "Open to see what's needed". Empty: "Nothing needs you here." The old cap (1 to 3 by window height) is gone, so the panel lists all of them and scrolls. Refresh: the task list re-reads every 60s while the window is visible, on window focus (if over 10s old) and when a room is opened (if over 10s old), on top of the existing visibility trigger. That is what the stale Haiku-gateway item needed.
6. **Consistency.** Same name per node in tree, header crumbs (`Uni › Techne › COhere`), title, footer ("All of COhere's work") and placeholder; the channel appears once as `#cohere`. A test asserts no `boulder/cohere` text in the DOM.

- A room with no node (a DM, a stray channel) still opens as the plain chat view with no sidebar; there is no node to put in the tree. Reachable from the Rooms drawer, which stays. Changing that means putting the tree around every chat.
- The Rooms drawer's flat list is still there (rail "Rooms"); it names rooms as the tree does but is a second index. Left until Aaron says to drop it.
- The Map, Octopus and Classic home layouts keep their old behavior (rooms open as plain rooms there; the resolver only applies to the Rooms layout).
- Uni-sketches walk shots not regenerated.
