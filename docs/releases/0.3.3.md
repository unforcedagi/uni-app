## Uni 0.3.3

For you shows only what is live and fresh.

- **Nothing older than a week.** An ask stays in For you for 7 days after its room asked it, or last confirmed it still holds. After that it goes back to its room, which decides whether to ask again with fresh context or close it. For you doesn't list those; it says how many there are, in one quiet line under Yours: "3 waiting on their rooms".
- **Only rooms that are in use.** An ask never says it goes to an archived room. A room that was archived resolves to the room its work moved to, else the ask's own place's room; an ask with no live room at all isn't shown.
- **Only places that are live.** Nothing from a place that is archived, resting or done.
