## Uni 0.3.2

Tap a card, see where it stands.

- **A card opens from wherever its name shows.** In For you (an ask's card, a line under Moving), in the stream (a card's post and a message that starts with the card's name carry a small "Glaze Tests · card" tag), and in an area (its cards under its stream's row, and in its room's details).
- **The card says where it stands.** Its one-sentence stand and when it was written, the hill (uphill: still figuring out; downhill: making), its last lines (one per session, newest first; your own answers marked "You", in quotes), what is open on it for you (answer right there, as in For you), and its threads. Six lines show; "N earlier" opens the rest. "Open its thread in #room" goes to the post your answers land under.
- **An area lists its cards.** The active ones first (yours, then ready to look at, then moving), the resting ones folded under "N resting".
- **States by shape, never colour alone.** A filled dot: something on it waits on you; half-filled: ready to look at; a ring: moving; a dash: resting. The Daylight reads the same in grey.
- **Side by side on a wide screen.** On the Daylight and a desktop the card opens beside the list, and the list stays in use: tap another card's name and the panel shows that one. On a phone it is a sheet from the bottom.
- **A private area's card shows only what it already says:** its lines as stored, its topics, and how many things wait on you; never the words of an ask.
