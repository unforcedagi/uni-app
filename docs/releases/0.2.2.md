## Uni 0.2.2

Asks act in their own room, and the rest of the app catches up with 0.2.

- **An ask goes to its own room.** Tap something under Needs you: its room comes forward with the ask open over it. "Say it in #room" puts the words in that room's box, ready for you to send. The app never asks you which room.
- **No dead rows.** An ask that sits at the top of your places used to do nothing when tapped. It opens now.
- **Opens on the home.** A fresh start opens on Uni's home, not in whichever room was busiest.
- **For you is gone.** Asks live in their rooms and under Needs you.
- **No more "Pass to Uni".** Send a journal entry to one of your rooms instead.
- **Search's fallback asks in the room you were in.** When your vault can't be reached, "Ask Uni in #room" names the room it will ask in.
- **Mail problems:** ask in #systems.
