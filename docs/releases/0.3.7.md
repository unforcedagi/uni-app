## Uni 0.3.7

Cards that need your tap show up while the app is open, and releases now wait for your tap.

- **A waiting card is not lost.** While the app is open, a card that needs your tap shows even if a read fails or the connection drops; a failed sync is tried again on its own.
- **Cards say which account first.** A Cloudflare or GitHub card's first line is the broker's own words: the account, and whether it holds production.
- **Releases wait for you.** From this release on, the Android app is signed only after you approve one card, "Sign Uni X.Y.Z for Android". The signing key stays in the broker.
- **Less to load.** The home no longer reads the retired Today roll-up.
