## Uni 0.2

One home, fewer buttons, the same calm look on every screen.

- **One home.** Uni and All are one place now. Tap Uni: the day in one sentence at the top, every room's latest talk under it, one box to write in at the bottom.
- **Pull the sentence down** to see what needs you, For you, To post and all your places. Tap a place and its room comes forward. Back puts the list away.
- **Fewer tabs.** The bar is Uni, Journal, Mail and More, on a phone and now on a tablet too. All and For you are no longer tabs of their own.
- **Fewer buttons.** No more "← Uni" on Mail, the Journal, the Vault, Calendar, Messages, To post and the Arcade: the bar is the way out. A note keeps Back and Edit, and the rest sits under one ⋯. The Vault has one New note. On a phone, the latest reply shows Try again, 👍 and Reply; hold a message for the rest.
- **One kind of header.** Each place has its name on the left and at most one small button on the right.
- **Easier to read on the Daylight.** Times, hints and small marks are a little darker, so they hold up in black and white.
- **Small moves.** The Journal's vault setting is now in Settings, under Developer. Messages' Sign out is at the foot of the list. Claude usage is in Settings.
