## Uni 0.3.4

For you, steadier.

- **For you no longer empties while the rooms load.** Until the app knows which rooms are live, your asks stay listed; none is counted as "waiting on its room" just because the room list isn't in yet.
- **A wrong date can't keep an ask forever.** An ask dated in the future counts from when it was made, so it still goes back to its room after a week.
- **Rooms and places are matched more carefully.** A reply room stored as an id is found; a project deep inside a resting area is treated as resting too.
