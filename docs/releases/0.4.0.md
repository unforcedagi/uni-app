## Uni 0.4.0

A new shape: home is your rooms, and what wants you is in the room it came from.

- **Home is your rooms.** Each row shows what is moving there and what wants you, with its buttons inline. Approval cards about to expire are pinned on top with a countdown.
- **A room says what is moving and what waits.** A thin strip under the room's name shows the run at work (tap it for the run's story) and what waits on you (tap to jump to it).
- **Asks are messages in their room.** Each one has its buttons, and Seen, right in the chat.
- **Private rooms keep their words.** An ask in a private room shows in full inside that room, where you answer it. Home only marks that something waits there (and says how many more wait in private places this device doesn't show), and an approval from a private room is pinned without its words.
- **Back returns to the room you were in.** From an applet or the journal, Back brings the room back where you were reading, with your draft.
- **The journal is its own place.**
- **One drawer for applets.** Arcade, Studio, To Post, Calendar and Vault, plus the doors to Mail and Messages. Recipes has a slot that says it isn't built yet. A note in your vault tagged `applet` joins the drawer as a sealed applet, and asks before it runs.
- **Search starts where you are.** Matches are grouped by place, other places with matches are listed apart, and when nothing is found you can ask Uni.
- **Arcade's "look here" links open in the app**, on the pinned card.
- **Fewer reads.** Asks, runs and the rest of the vault's lists are read through one reader on one timer, so the app asks the vault less often.
- **New screens without a new APK.** This app carries the key for signed screen updates. From now on, a change to the screens alone can reach your phone over the air after one tap (at most one a day); it goes in at the next start, or when you come back after a few minutes away. Settings says which screens are running. Changes to the app itself still come as an APK.
- **Gone:** For you, the task and work lists, the areas tree, the cards and projects views, and the All stream. Asks are now messages in their room; what was in the task and project views is now each room's strip, which says what is moving and what waits.
