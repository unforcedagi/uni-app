## Uni 0.3.0

For you is the top of the home.

- **Three bands.** Yours: what only you can answer, the one whose default comes first at the top, five at a time ("3 more" holds the rest). Ready to look at: finished things to taste. Moving: what the cards did since you last looked; quiet cards are counted, not listed.
- **Every item says where it's from, why now, and what happens if you wait.** The card and its area (tap it to open the card: where it stands, the hill, the last lines). The default and its date ("Celadon · Fri 5pm"), the other options beside it, or "No default: waits for you".
- **One tap answers.** It takes the default (or the option you tap), closes the ask with your answer, and posts it as you in the ask's room, under the card's post. A post that doesn't go says so, with Retry; your answer is kept either way.
- **Hold to talk** on any item: speak, let go, read what it heard, Send.
- **Needs you is gone from the drawer.** For you replaces it; the drawer keeps the approvals waiting, To post and your places. A room's details still list what waits on you there.
- **Old decisions still work**: picking one decides it as before, and says the pick in its room.
