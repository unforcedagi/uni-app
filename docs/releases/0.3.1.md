## Uni 0.3.1

The stream gets the screen back, in every area.

- **For you folds to one line.** "3 for you · 1 to look at · 2 moving", and under it the first ask. Tap it to open the bands; everything they did before (one tap takes a default, hold to talk, the card) works the same. When nothing is yours it says so, with the day's quiet line; when something couldn't be checked it says that, with Try again. A post that didn't go keeps its Retry in sight even when folded.
- **The stream has most of the screen.** The big sentence at the top is gone; For you's line says it now. The places are still one tap away ("Places" under the date, with how many are at work: "Two at work · Places").
- **Every area has its stream.** A row over the stream: All, then each area. Tap an area to see its rooms woven by time (its own room and the rooms of the places inside it), with For you showing only that area's asks. Tap All (or Back, or Escape) to see every room again.
- **The area's room opens from its stream** (its name with "Open", under the row), and Back from the room returns to that area's stream.
