## Uni 0.5.0

Every ask is one card, answers go in the main line, and Mail is rebuilt around the job. This is a full app install, not new screens over the air: the app itself changed.

- **One ask, one card.** An ask from a room, a task that needs you and an approval waiting for your tap all look and act the same: one card, in the room where it was asked, on home, and in the bar's count. A task that only says "tap the card" is that card, so it shows once. A card opens in place to its context: who asked, why now, the default and until when.
- **Answers are flat.** Reply in the room puts your words in the room's main line with the ask quoted above them, never in a thread, and the ask folds to "You answered". Answers to tasks post in the main line too.
- **Ask buttons say what they do.** I did it, Seen, Later (with its day), and Reply in the room. Seen and Archive end an ask for good, and an ask that runs out leaves on its own.
- **Copy on long press.** Hold a message's words to select and copy them. Its actions (Copy text, Save as note and the rest) sit in a sheet beside the words.
- **Mail v2.** Mail opens on your Inbox, and every place is a tab, as in Messages: Later, Waiting, Drafts, Sent, Archive, and Uni's own mail. What you've read folds to one line. Uni's draft waits at the foot of the thread, and Reply opens it right under the message it answers; you edit it and send it yourself. Search runs as you type, and Files lists only mail with attachments. Archive or Later moves you on to the next thread. Anything Uni wants to send for you waits under Waiting until you say yes.
- **Applets ask once, for exactly what they use.** An applet names everything it reaches in one manifest and asks for it on one card; anything it didn't ask for is refused. A sealed applet may add and change notes in the folders it names, in its own vault, and applets come from every vault. A Recipes applet in a recipes vault takes the drawer's Recipes slot.
- **Fixes.** A journal answer with a % in its first words is saved. On a phone, a note like "Copied" no longer covers the message box in a room.
- **Gone:** the separate ask sheet and the approval cards pinned over home (cards sit in their rooms now); Mail's mailbox column, All inboxes, the You/Uni tag on every row, Mail's own address bar and the Waiting banner.
