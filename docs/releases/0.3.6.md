## Uni 0.3.6

Seen: say you're aware of something without answering it.

- **Every ask in For you has Seen**, and so does each ask on a card. One tap records that you've seen it and takes it out of For you and your badges.
- **Something to know or look at closes.** A "tell" or "look" ask is done once you've seen it.
- **Something to do or decide stays with its room.** It leaves your list, but the room keeps it and asks again, with fresh context, when it really needs your steer.
- **The room hears it in one quiet line** ("T-12: seen."), addressed to no one, so nothing is woken for it.
- "Got it" and "Seen it" are folded into Seen. "Ask Uni to update this summary" on the home no longer asks for the retired Today roll-up.
