## Uni 0.3.5

One number for what needs you.

- **The home's count and the places' badges agree.** What needs you is now worked out once, by Uni, and written on each ask: an open ask of yours, on a card, asked or confirmed by its room in the last week, in a place that is live. For you, the badges on your places, the dots in the tree and the room's list all count that, so they always say the same number.
- **Nothing old sneaks back in.** The app no longer keeps its own rules about what is yours (a three-day check, old decision notes, a session waiting on an answer). If Uni hasn't marked something as needing you, it isn't counted.
- **Private rooms follow Uni's one room list.** An ask whose room is private there (under its current or an old name) stays a count, never words, wherever it is filed.
